Could Diesel Be Tracked from Iran to Russia?

A first-iteration investigation using Sayari Graph trade data to examine petroleum-product shipments, company relationships, and possible regional routing.

Research question Method Findings Evidence Limitations

Research question

Can Sayari Graph identify diesel or petroleum-product trade relationships connecting Iran and Russia?

The goal was to determine whether the available trade records showed a direct Iran-to-Russia shipment, an indirect route through another country, or only a broader company-level relationship.

Research method

I used the Trade search in Sayari Graph and examined shipment records rather than relying only on company names or aggregate country lists.

Product category Diesel and petroleum products
HS code 2710.19
Shipper country Iran
Receiver country Russia

For each record, I reviewed the product description, shipper, receiver, country of manufacture, country of departure, country of arrival, shipment identifier, and source record.

Findings

Important finding: One reviewed shipment involved an Iranian shipper and a product described as Diesel fuel K-4, but the listed arrival country was Uzbekistan rather than Russia. Therefore, this record is not direct evidence of an Iran-to-Russia shipment.

The search returned approximately 925 results using the selected criteria. The results indicate trade relationships involving Iranian shippers, Russian-linked records, and other countries in the region.

However, the result count alone does not prove that every shipment traveled directly from Iran to Russia. Individual shipment records must be checked separately.

Documented facts versus inferences

Documented facts

  • Sayari lists the shipment under HS code 2710.19.
  • The product description includes Diesel fuel K-4.
  • The shipper address is in Mashhad, Iran.
  • The shipment's listed arrival country is Uzbekistan.
  • Russia appears in the broader country relationship information for the shipper.

Inference

The data suggests that the Iranian company participates in a broader regional petroleum-trade network. However, the reviewed shipment does not prove that this particular shipment traveled from Iran to Russia.

Limitations

  • HS code 2710.19 covers petroleum products and does not necessarily prove that every result is diesel.
  • A company’s country relationship list does not prove that every shipment traveled between those countries.
  • A shipper’s address is not necessarily the same as the physical departure point.
  • The results do not, by themselves, prove sanctions violations or illegal activity.
  • Trade records may be incomplete or use translated product descriptions.

Conclusion

The Sayari search demonstrates how trade data can be used to investigate possible petroleum-product routes and relationships involving Iran, Russia, and intermediary countries.

The current evidence supports an Iran-linked regional trade network. Additional shipment records would be needed to confirm a direct Iran-to-Russia diesel route.
