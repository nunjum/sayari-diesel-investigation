# sayari-diesel-investigation
# Could Diesel Be Tracked from Iran to Russia?

## Research question

Can Sayari Graph identify trade relationships involving diesel or
petroleum products moving from Iran to Russia?

## Tool

This investigation was conducted using Sayari Graph's Trade search.

## Search method

I searched using:

- Product category: diesel / petroleum products
- HS code: 2710.19
- Shipper country: Iran
- Receiver country: Russia

I reviewed the shipment records for:

- Product description
- HS code
- Shipper
- Receiver
- Country of manufacture
- Country of departure
- Country of arrival
- Shipment identifier
- Shipment source record

## Findings

Sayari returned approximately **925 results** using the selected search
criteria.

The results show relationships between Iranian shippers and records
associated with Russian receivers. However, each shipment must be checked
individually to determine whether it represents a direct Iran-to-Russia
route.

One shipment I reviewed involved:

- Shipper: Gohar Negar Toos
- Shipper address: Mashhad, Iran
- Product: Diesel fuel K-4
- HS code: 2710.19
- Country of arrival: Uzbekistan

Because that shipment arrived in Uzbekistan, it is not direct evidence of
a diesel shipment from Iran to Russia.

## Documented facts versus inferences

### Documented facts

- Sayari lists the shipment under HS code 2710.19.
- The product description includes diesel fuel K-4.
- The shipper address is in Mashhad, Iran.
- The shipment's listed arrival country is Uzbekistan.
- Russia appears in the broader country relationship list for the shipper.

### Inference

The results suggest that the Iranian company participates in a broader
regional petroleum-trade network that includes Iran, Uzbekistan, Russia,
and other countries.

However, the reviewed shipment does not prove that this particular
shipment traveled from Iran to Russia.

## Conclusion

The Sayari results demonstrate how trade data can be used to investigate
possible routes and relationships involving Iranian petroleum products
and Russian-linked trade activity.

The current evidence supports an Iran-linked regional trade network.
Additional shipment records would be needed to confirm a direct
Iran-to-Russia diesel route.

## Limitations

- HS code 2710.19 covers petroleum products and does not always prove that
  every shipment was diesel.
- A company's country relationship list does not prove that every shipment
  traveled between those countries.
- A shipper's address is not necessarily the same as the physical
  shipment departure point.
- The results do not, by themselves, prove sanctions violations or
  illegal activity.
- Trade records may be incomplete or use translated product descriptions.
